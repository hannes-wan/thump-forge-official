## Why Thump Forge exists

Thump Forge is a native desktop bass amp simulator and VST3 effects rack built for bassists, producers, and mix engineers who want tight sub-bass weight, controllable midrange grit, and instant routing visibility on Windows x64 and Linux x64.

> **Edition boundary:** The technical sections below describe the complete product surface and can include optional licensed workflows. The exact free Community Edition scope is listed separately below.

### One bass rack from bedroom practice to final DAW print

Thump Forge puts twelve dedicated bass processors, visual routing, and honest headroom metering into a single native desktop app and zero-latency VST3 plugin.

### Reorderable 12-module bass rack

Drag Gate, Comp, Octave, Envelope, Drive, Preamp, EQ, Chorus, Cab, Delay, Reverb, and Limiter into any order while the right-hand Signal flow map tracks every active stage.

### Parallel compression and four preamp voicings

Blend dry string attack against compressed sustain on an interactive knee curve, then drive Clean DI, Warm Tube, Vintage SVT, or Modern preamp tone stacks with protected sub-bass.

### Four-band bass EQ and cabinet color

Carve Low 60 Hz, Low mid 250 Hz, High mid 900 Hz, High 4 kHz, and a sweepable high-pass filter over a live spectrum display, then finish with five bass cabinet voicings.

### Standalone app, zero-latency VST3, and 5-string tuner

Practice with the standalone app, Demo riff audition loop, and pre-effects BEADG tuner, or drop the zero-latency VST3 plugin into a DAW track with project-embedded recall.

### Protect the low end while adding harmonic cut

- Blend dry attack with compressed body inside Comp using an interactive knee curve and live VU gain-reduction meter.
- Switch between Clean DI, Warm Tube, Vintage SVT, and Modern preamp voicings with sub-bass crossover protection so grit tightens the mids without collapsing the fundamentals.
- Anchor peaks with a movable Limiter pedal anywhere in the chain plus master Output Level and Peak guard at the end of the rack.

### Identical tone in standalone practice and DAW sessions

- Run the native standalone application on Windows x64 or Linux x64 with selectable sample rates and low-latency audio buffers.
- Drop the zero-latency VST3 plugin onto any bass track; every DAW project stores the full rack state independently without overwriting your personal Voices library.
- Use the built-in Demo riff to audition voices and refine EQ before plugging in a bass, and check tuning any time on the pre-effects 5-string BEADG tuner.

### Full rack included in Community Edition, no subscription

- Download Community Edition free with the standalone app, VST3 plugin, all twelve modules, ten factory voices, 5-string tuner, Demo riff, and three reusable custom voice slots.
- Overwrite or delete any of the three free slots whenever your setlist changes, with confirmation prompts that guard against accidental loss.

## Frequently asked questions

> **Community scope:** Community Edition includes the standalone app, VST3 plugin, all 12 bass modules, 10 factory voices, 5-string tuner, and 3 free custom preset slots; unlimited custom voices require a licensed edition.

<details>
<summary><strong>What is Thump Forge and which operating systems does it support?</strong></summary>

Thump Forge is a native bass amp simulator and 12-module effects rack built for Windows x64 and Linux x64. It delivers tight low-end processing through parallel compression, four preamp voicings, four-band spectrum EQ, cabinet color, and output peak limiting.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does Thump Forge include both a standalone app and a DAW VST3 plugin?</strong></summary>

Yes. Both the standalone application and the VST3 plugin run the exact same zero-latency DSP chain and UI. The standalone app connects directly to your audio interface for practice, while the VST3 plugin embeds its full rack state inside your DAW project.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which bass modules and factory voices are built into the rack?</strong></summary>

The rack includes twelve reorderable modules: Gate, Comp, Octave, Envelope, Drive, Preamp, EQ, Chorus, Cab, Delay, Reverb, and Limiter, plus master Peak guard. Ten editable factory voices—from Studio DI and Motown Round to SVT Crunch and Funk Filter—are ready on launch.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I audition tones before plugging a bass into my computer?</strong></summary>

Yes. The standalone app includes a built-in Demo riff button in the top bar that loops a bass line directly through your active signal chain. You can compare factory voices, drag modules, and dial EQ or preamp grit immediately, then stop the riff to return to live input.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does the built-in tuner track a 5-string bass low B accurately?</strong></summary>

Yes. The built-in Tuner reads the dry instrument input before effects across 30 Hz to 400 Hz with a 3-cent in-tune window and clear B-E-A-D-G reference indicators, making it reliable for both 4-string EADG and 5-string BEADG bass guitars.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which Thump Forge installer includes the DAW VST3 plugin?</strong></summary>

Both the Windows x64 direct installer (.exe) and the Microsoft Store edition deploy the standalone app and the zero-latency Windows VST3 plugin, and the Linux x64 DEB, RPM, and AppImage packages include both the standalone bass rig and Linux VST3 support.

</details>
